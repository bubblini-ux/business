### `#2026110` — Apps Shopping & Commerce Ecosystem
- **Tag:** `#2026110 apps shopping`
- **Category:** Mobile & Web Commerce Applications
- **Summary:** Unified shopping application suite integrating curated product discovery, affiliate attribution, price comparisons, and seamless checkout flows.
- **Core Capabilities:**
  - **Smart Catalog & Discovery:** Personalized shopping feeds, wishlist collections, and cross-merchant search.
  - **Affiliate & Referral Attribution:** Deep-link routing and conversion tracking tied to the [Affiliate Marketing Vault](./Affiliate-Marketing-Vault/Index.md).
  - **Frictionless Checkout:** One-click payment processing via Stripe (`PaymentIntents`, subscriptions, digital wallets).
  - **Merchant & Inventory Sync:** Headless commerce back end compatible with composable engines (e.g., Medusa, custom REST/GraphQL APIs).
  - **Analytics & Retention:** Conversion funnel metrics, price-drop alerts, and automated email/push re-engagement.
